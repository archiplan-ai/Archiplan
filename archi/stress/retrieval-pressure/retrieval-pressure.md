# Retrieval pressure

Twelfth round, pressing the search design before code exists: the intent wants one verb
that turns a phrase into addresses across every KB object, and v0010 models it as a
single scan — no index, no service, no second store. This round hunts what breaks that
shape: the index the design refused (and the staleness it would have bought), the common
word that floods a naive ranking, the morphology gap between a query's phrasing and the
corpus's, the broken tree at exactly the moment search matters most, the hit that
answers with a dead end, the KB that outgrows a linear scan, and the archive that can
only answer for the model's half of yesterday.
