# Two rounds, one record

Two writers each open a stress round on their own branch — same session name by convention, or
different names. Both cases merged for real.

## Attractor

The round record — the durable why — is authored by the merge instead of a writer.

## Resolution

Broke, silently where it hurts. Different names: git merges without a sound (different
folders), and only the post-merge check raises `E_SESSION: both open` — correctly named and
located, at the desk of whoever merged; the fold that follows is two hand file-ops that work
but leave no record and delete a charter. Same name: one add/add conflict on the session file,
the natural keep-a-charter resolution, and then everything is green — one open session carrying
both writers' stressors under one writer's charter. The save closes it as a single round and
the incidence report solemnly records pressure nobody designed; the other charter survives only
as git archaeology, and the unresolved verdicts land on the integrator as findings. Folding two
rounds into one is sometimes exactly right — today it happens by accident, leaves no trace, and
cannot be done on purpose. Derived: rounds-fold-deliberately.
