# Authoring pressure

Fifteenth round, and the last of the bootstrap's open issues come home as pressure rather
than prose. The surface under test is the one the whole model was authored through: a
requirement binds to reality two ways — a `Satisfy` bullet to a test, `satisfied-by` to a
model element — and the retired `issues/` folder proved both bindings quietly under-serve
their author. A verification bullet voids on an ASCII hyphen with no word said; a satisfaction
that honestly pins one port launders through the whole owning node because the resolver
descends only nodes. Neither errors, both degrade the record the tool exists to keep honest.
Two stressors, both replays, pinned to v0012.
