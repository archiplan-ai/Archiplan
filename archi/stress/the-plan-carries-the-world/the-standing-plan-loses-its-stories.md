# The standing plan loses its stories

Every plan in this repository has a `scenarios.md` holding free text, written under the
old rule. The new rule says the plan authors none and collects them from the world. On a
tree with no world facts, that makes the collected block empty, and an empty block means
the plan skips the step and closes directly. Twenty-six plan folders hold stories that
no verb will read again, and the completed ones hold latches stamped over a block that
the current rule says never existed.

## Attractor

The stories go silent rather than migrate. Nobody deletes them, because deleting reads
as losing work; nobody reads them, because no verb prints them. They become the folder
of prose that everybody steps around — the exact fate the world was built to prevent for
world facts, arriving first for the plans' own stories.

## Resolution

A clean break with a finding, not a fallback: derived `a-plan-s-own-scenarios-block-retires`.
No verb reads `scenarios.md` and no verb deletes it. An open plan holding one raises
`unmigrated_scenarios` so a person meets the file while that plan is still live; a
completed plan keeps its block as the record of a ceremony that ran under the rule of its
day. The migration cannot be mechanical — naming the condition under a story is judgment —
so the finding is the whole mechanism. The price is signed in `the-old-stories-migrate-by-hand`.
