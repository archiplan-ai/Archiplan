# The bridge has no verb to walk it

Ask the question the wing was built to answer: what conditions this node? `covers` is
declared the only reliable bridge, because a fact is written without the nouns of the
model and lexical search cannot cross that gap — `the-wing-is-reached-by-traversal` signs
that trade. Then look for the verb that walks it. `world` does `add` and `rm`. `search`
matches phrases. `query` composes model slices and knows nothing of docs. The traversal
the whole design leans on exists only as a person opening files and reading frontmatter.

## Attractor

The bridge is declared and never built, so the wing is reached the one way it was
designed not to be reached: by phrase, badly. An operator asks `archi search` about a
node, gets architecture hits and no facts, and concludes the wing holds nothing about it.
The decision that traded findability for simplicity paid the price and never collected
the thing it bought.

## Resolution

`archi world ls`, narrowed by `--covers <node>`: derived `one-verb-walks-the-bridge`. The
listing lives on the `world` verb and not on `query`, which composes model slices and
holds no doc. This closes the debt `the-wing-is-reached-by-traversal` opened: that
decision spent findability by phrase on the promise that the traversal is exact, and the
traversal was a convention until now.
