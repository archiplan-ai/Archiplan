# The wing replaced something that worked

The plan's scenarios worked. Free text on the envelope, printed at the close, latched,
gone with the plan — twenty-six plans used it and none of them complained. The wing
replaced it with collected Gherkin, a drift report, an out-of-plan mark, member tags, a
coverage gate and a hand migration for every open plan. Count what the replacement bought
against what it cost: the block outlives the plan, it parses, and it carries a reason.

## Attractor

The exchange is defended by properties nobody asked for. The stories died with the plan and
that was fine, because a plan is a unit of work and its stories were about that work. What
now outlives the plan is a corpus somebody has to keep true, and the cost of keeping it true
is the cost the wing already admits it cannot pay by machine. A working cheap thing was
traded for an expensive thing whose central weakness is known.

## Resolution

Accepted, and the exchange stands as round two set it: the stories live with the facts that
condition them. What this round did take back is the part of the trade nobody had agreed to
pay — the migration. Old plans are left exactly as they are, silent and unconverted, under
`the-old-plans-are-left-alone`, and the new rule applies only forward. The move itself is
signed in `the-stories-move-out-of-the-plan`.
