# Six rounds added and none subtracted

Read the round records in order. Every resolution derives a requirement; not one retires
one. Six requirements became thirty-one, the break rate climbed from three in five to five
in five, and each round's fixes were the next round's surface. The tool's own scoring says
the landscape held — K̄ 1.77 to 1.84, regime unchanged — because the growth landed in
documents, where no instrument in this repository measures it.

## Attractor

The spiral has no exit condition. A round that presses a larger design finds more, so the
canon's rule — repeat until a round survives — selects for designs that grew until the
author ran out of patience rather than for designs that got harder to break. The stopping
point is fatigue, and fatigue arrives after the spec is too large to build and before
anybody has written a line of code.

## Resolution

Accepted: no requirement can carry this, because the pressure is on the ritual and not
on the system. What the round did instead is the answer in kind. One requirement retired, one migration dropped with its
decision, two decisions replaced by their reversals. The count goes from thirty-one to
twenty-nine with the largest implementation item removed, and the record shows which round
reversed which.

The deeper reading stands and is worth keeping: a round that presses a larger design finds
more, so "repeat until a round survives" selects for growth unless a round is aimed at
subtraction. That aim is not a rule this repository can enforce — it is what a seventh round
did once, on a design that had stopped converging.
