# The world under its own weight

Five rounds added to the world and none subtracted. It now carries six finding kinds, two
retrieval paths, three pre-flights on one removal, and a claim that its scenarios are
executable which no verb has ever tested by running one. The round asks what the world
costs when it works: whether its own reports crowd the worklist they belong to, whether
the ceremony that latches on a scenario knows the scenario passed, whether the two ways
to reach a fact agree, whether clearing a fact for removal is a walk or a wall, and
whether anything at all teaches an operator that the world exists.
