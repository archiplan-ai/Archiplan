# the swap under hostile ground

The self-update flow is new ground: the first verb that rewrites the
tool itself, on machines the project does not control. This round
presses the swap where the ground is hostile — the binary is not the
installed one, the process dies mid-move, the server's idea of latest
disagrees with the client's arithmetic.
