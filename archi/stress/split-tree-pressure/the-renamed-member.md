# The renamed member

The backend team renames their repository, and someone helpfully renames the member in the
manifest to match. A thousand journal events still say the old name.

## Attractor

The journal's refs orphan wholesale: verify grades a wall of Missing, capture mints fresh links
under the new name beside the stale ones, and the lineage splits in two.

## Resolution

Survives because the name is declared identity, not a mirror of the remote: the url absorbs
remote renames without touching anything, and an undeclared name in the journal resolves to a
loud unknown-member diagnostic naming the recovery — restore the declaration. A journal
migration verb stays deferred until demanded; failing loud is the design holding.
