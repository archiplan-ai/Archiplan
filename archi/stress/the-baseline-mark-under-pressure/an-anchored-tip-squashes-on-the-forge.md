# An anchored tip squashes on the forge

The gate forces anchoring the seat tips; the member's PR then squash-merges —
the anchored sha never becomes an ancestor of the member's main.

## Attractor

Every squash-shop's next mint refuses the auto-base and the gate reads as
ceremony that anchored a commit history forgot.

## Resolution

Held — the refusal is the designed escape, not a dead end: the next mint
answers with candidate branches and `--base <member>=<branch>`, and the fresh
seat re-anchors from the pulled main first thing (the mark then names the
squashed state). The recorded risk stands: merge-commit or rebase integration
keeps the auto-path; squash degrades it to one explicit question.
