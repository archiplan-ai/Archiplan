# The world meets the verbs and the seats

Three rounds built the world and joined it to the plan. Nothing yet pressed the surface an
operator actually touches. The world declares `covers` the only bridge from a node to what
conditions it, and offers no verb that walks it. The verb it does offer has never met an
unbound checkout or a refusal contract. Two branches that both edit the world meet at a
merge whose fold ceremony knows only stress rounds. A fact reaches across member
repositories that the plan resolves one at a time. And the link that was supposed to make
a scenario machine-decided anchors on step text that anyone may reword.

This round presses the world where hands land: the verbs, the seats and the code.
