# Leftovers stay loud

The filter that quiets the claimed cross-product must not quiet the unclaimed motion: a changed
item no in-flight task claims is the plan failing to account for code, and that signal is the
audit's teeth inside the wave.

## Attractor

Press the boundary between suppression and leftovers. A changed file outside every task's
outputs — whatever its terms, however unrelated to any spec_ref — must still be reported as a
leftover by capture, exactly as before the filter. Suppression applies to (claimed item, ref)
pairs; leftovers are items with no claimant at all, upstream of any pairing.

## Resolution

Survives: the leftover branch runs before spec_refs are consulted — an unclaimed change short-
circuits to the report and the signal filter never sees it. The filter cannot eat what it is
never fed.
