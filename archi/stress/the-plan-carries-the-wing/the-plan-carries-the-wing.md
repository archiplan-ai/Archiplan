# The plan carries the wing

The last round joined the wing to the plan and the joint has never been pressed. A plan
pins a version; the facts it now collects live in files the archive does not seal, so
the block can move under a plan in flight. Every plan standing today authored stories
the new rule says it may not author. A task names facts that a verb can retire while a
wave runs, and that verb pre-flights links and dependants but knows nothing of plans.
And a fact that covers four nodes joins a plan that holds one of them, bringing a story
that walks through three nodes nobody is building. This round presses the seam, not the
wing.
