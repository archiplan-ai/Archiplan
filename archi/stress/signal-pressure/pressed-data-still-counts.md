# Pressed data still counts

A stressor legitimately presses a data term — corrupt the `LinkEvent` journal, poison the
`DefTable`, tamper with a `CanonicalRender` — and the round's matrix must show it: hotspot
density, coupling pairs, compound surfaces, all of it.

## Attractor

The filter overreaches: "data nodes are noise" hardens into dropping data *columns*, and a
tampering round scores as if its pressure landed nowhere — the matrix thins, hotspots on stores
vanish, and the analysis goes blind exactly where integrity pressure concentrates.

## Resolution

Holds on v0004 — data columns participate fully today — and fences the fix: only the
under-stressed *emission* consults the class filter; column construction, the matrix, hotspots,
coupling and compound findings never do. A pressed data term is behavior by demonstration,
whatever its classification; the sweep only mutes the *never-pressed* vocabulary, and
`--all-terms` restores even that. Pinned by a regression: a matrix with a pressed and an
unpressed data term keeps the pressed column everywhere and mutes only the unpressed one's info
line.
