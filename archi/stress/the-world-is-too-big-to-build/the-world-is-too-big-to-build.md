# The world is too big to build

Six rounds ran and the break rate rose: three of five, three, four, four, five, five. Six
requirements became thirty-one. Every round added mechanism, mechanism added surface, and
surface produced the next round's breaks — a spiral, not a convergence. Five rounds pressed
whether the world works. This one presses whether it should be this large: what the first
fact costs the person who writes it, what the world repeats that the tree already holds, what
it replaced that was working, and whether anything in six rounds was ever taken away.
