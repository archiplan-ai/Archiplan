# The world under pressure

The world landed on paper and never met a day of use. It presses `DocsCompiler`,
already the hotspot the last landscape named, and it adds two terms nothing has
tested — `WorldDoc` and its grammar. The round asks the questions the happy path
skips. A fact is built to die: what happens to the code that its scenarios reached?
The grammar is a deliberate subset: what happens when a real feature file arrives?
The `covers` list resolves against the live model: what happens when a rename moves
the model while the world stands still? And the world works only while it stays small
enough to re-read, which nothing in the design enforces.
