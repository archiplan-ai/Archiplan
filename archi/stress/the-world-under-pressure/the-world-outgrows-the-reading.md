# The world outgrows the reading

Run the world for a year. Two hundred facts stand. The design rests on one assumption
that no file states: a person can read the whole world in one sitting and ask of each
fact whether it still holds. Nothing bounds the count, nothing reports the growth,
and no field records when a fact was last confirmed against the world. The assumption
was load-bearing and it was never written down.

## Attractor

Nobody re-reads. A fact stays because no mechanism removes it, `check` stays green
over prose that stopped being true years ago, and the world keeps its authority while
losing its warrant. The failure the world was built to make visible — right system,
absent situation — reappears inside the world itself, one level up.

## Resolution

`check` closes with the count of standing facts and the count of them with no
sources: derived `the-check-counts-the-world`. The assumption stays an assumption —
nothing bounds the world and nothing claims to know whether a fact is true — but it
stops being invisible. No field joins the header, so the three lists stay three.
