# Nothing teaches the wing

An operator upgrades and gets a green tree, a silent wing and a new verb nobody mentioned.
`the-wing-arrives-without-noise` puts discovery on the briefing, and no requirement in this
repository owns what the briefing says. The workflow skill that drives the whole spec loop
— capture intent, derive requirements, model, stress, version — has no step where a world
fact is written, so an agent following it faithfully produces a spec with an empty wing and
never notices.

## Attractor

The wing is complete, checkable, and unused. Every mechanism works and nothing invokes
them, because the procedure everybody actually follows was written before the wing existed
and still describes a two-step chain from intent to requirements. The most likely end state
is not a rotten wing but an empty one — and an empty wing is indistinguishable from a
project where nobody had anything to say about the world.

## Resolution

The briefing owns the wing: derived `the-briefing-puts-the-world-in-the-loop`. It names the
verb and the shape, states the content rule, and puts the capture step in the spec loop
before requirements are derived. This is the requirement whose absence would have made every
other one in this intent inert — six rounds of mechanism invoked by nobody, and an empty
wing that reads exactly like a project with nothing to say about the world.
