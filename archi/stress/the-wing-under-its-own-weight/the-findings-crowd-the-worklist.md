# The findings crowd the worklist

Write one honest early fact: a condition observed in a conversation, the model not yet
reaching it, no parent. It reports `world_ungrounded` if the conversation has no locator,
`world_uncovered` because `covers` is empty, and `world_speaks_the_model` if the
conditioning paragraph happens to use a word that is also an element name. Three findings
on one healthy file, on a tree where nothing is wrong, out of six kinds the wing added in
five rounds.

## Attractor

Findings are the worklist in this tool, and a worklist that fires three lines per healthy
file stops being read. The operator learns that world findings are noise before they ever
meet a real one, and `world_ungrounded` — the single signal standing between the wing and
an agent's confabulation — goes past unread with the rest. The wing's instruments defeat
the wing's one defence.

## Resolution

The per-fact findings collapse into one: derived `one-fact-reports-one-finding`. A fact
reports a single `world_state` line carrying every state it holds, so an honest early fact
costs the reader one glance instead of three. The graph reports stay separate because they
are about the wing rather than about a fact.
