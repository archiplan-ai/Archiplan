# The wing is reached by traversal

A world fact is found from the node it covers, not from a phrase about the
architecture. Search reaches the wing, and its yield there is low on purpose.

The cost is real. A world fact is written without the nouns of the model — that rule
is what keeps it from becoming a requirement in costume — and the same rule makes its
vocabulary disjoint from the vocabulary of a query about the design. `archi search
"sandbox"` returns nothing on a tree full of worktrees today, and the world wing
widens that gap rather than closing it.

We take the cost. Closing it means matching by meaning, and meaning needs an index
that outlives the query — a second copy of the truth to keep alive, which
`agent-retrieval` already refused for the whole knowledge base. The `covers` list is
the bridge instead: exact, checkable, and blind to phrasing. To ask what conditions a
node is a traversal, and it needs no vocabulary at all.
