# The old plans are left alone

Every plan written before the world keeps its `scenarios.md`, no verb reads it, and no
finding names it. Those plans have no scenarios and that is the end of it.

The tree is knowingly inconsistent afterwards: twenty-six plans hold blocks that the
current rule says a plan may not author, and nothing in the tool says so. We accept that
rather than carry a migration or a finding. Converting a story into a world fact means
naming the condition underneath it, which is the whole judgement the world exists to ask
for — a converter would have written facts with empty sources and a sentence lifted from a
story, and a finding would have queued twenty-six pieces of that judgement as chores.

The rule applies forward instead, and there it is strict: a plan minted after the world
cannot close on an empty block. A plan whose nodes no fact covers is a plan whose subject
nobody has said anything true about, and the refusal names exactly that.
