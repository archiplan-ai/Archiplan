# A passing check ends on the landscape

A check with no errors closes on the landscape read: the scoring line — N, E, K̄, σ, P̄,
regime, kept bare, its symbol legend riding the agent briefing — and the refactoring
directions it implies, the highest-risk coupling targets and the corridor actions,
largest corridors first, capped so a sparse landscape's singleton corridors cannot flood
the report. Advisory findings do not withhold the read; an empty landscape (N = 0) earns
none; `--json` carries the same read as `nkp` — the report without its matrix and notes.
The knobs and the full report stay on `archi nkp`; the check's read is the default slice,
every time.

## System Context

The close of a check is the operator's steadiest moment of attention, and a coupling
number that must be asked for separately stops being watched — `coupling-is-measured`
claims the analyses, this claims their delivery.
