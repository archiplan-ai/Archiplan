# Accept is a signed break

A stressor's fourth outcome, `accepted`: the architecture bent and the break is
kept. Nothing derives — a requirement whose origin names an accepted stressor is
`E_DOC_REF`, the contradiction between the fork's two arms — and the sacrifice
lives entirely on a linked decision: an accepted stressor no decision links is the
`accepted_unjustified` finding, mirroring `breaking_unanswered` — a break is never
accepted silently. `Resolution` states the consequence being lived with; one
decision may sign several accepted stressors, and the record is correctable after
the fact like any text.

## System Context

An accepted break is still a break. The row stands in the incidence matrix under
its own outcome and presses on: it compounds with surviving and accepted partners
(`the-matrix-joins-stress-to-structure`), and a compound pair carrying an accepted
member names it and is flagged louder — the trade was signed against a founding
promise. Breaking rows stay out of compound analysis (their answers derived);
accepted rows never leave it, precisely because nothing was derived.
