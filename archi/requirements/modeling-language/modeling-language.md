# Modeling language

An architecture lives in whiteboards, wikis and heads — none of which can be diffed,
queried or checked. The tool's foundation is one structured substrate for describing
arbitrary systems: a free graph of nodes that expose ports and nest scopes, joined by
distinguished, typed edges, sliceable into views and layers, addressable without ambient
context, and changed only by whole-model rebuilds so integrity is a property of
construction rather than repair. Two front doors serve two audiences — diffable source
for authors, JSON statements for agents and compilers — over one semantic core, and the
machine layer's contract is as much a part of the language as its syntax: atomic batches,
idempotent restatement, machine-readable rejection.
